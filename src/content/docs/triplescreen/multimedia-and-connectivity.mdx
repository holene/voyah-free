# Multimedia and Connectivity

### Supported Media
- USB (MP3, FLAC, AAC, WAV)
- Bluetooth Audio
- DAB/FM/AM radio
- Dailymotion video streaming

> Video playback is disabled on the driver display while driving, but available on the passenger screen.

### Mobile Integration
Supports:
- **Apple CarPlay**
- **Android Auto**
- **Bluetooth calling & contacts**
