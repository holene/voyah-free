# Climate and Comfort

The **automatic dual-zone climate system** manages temperature, fan speed, and air quality.

- **AUTO mode:** full automatic control  
- **SYNC mode:** synchronises both zones  
- **Fragrance system:** injects scent via micro-pump  
- **IAQC:** monitors PM2.5 and switches to recirculation in poor air quality  
- **Seat controls:** heating, ventilation, and massage (3 levels each)
