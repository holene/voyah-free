# Why Does the Voyah Free Always Start in ECO Mode?

From **1 January 2025**, European carmakers must produce about **20% electric vehicles** to meet EU emission directives.  
ECO mode reduces the car’s energy consumption and thus the vehicle’s calculated CO₂ footprint.

Although Norway has nearly **100% renewable power**, global EU reporting includes energy source emissions.  
Starting in ECO mode helps manufacturers demonstrate lower average CO₂ per kilometre across markets.

**Key effects of ECO mode:**
- Reduced acceleration
- Lower A/C output
- Optimised power usage

> “Eco+” allows further reductions, disabling comfort features for maximum efficiency.

**Summary:**  
This default start mode helps manufacturers meet EU CO₂ targets and emission-credit pooling requirements.
