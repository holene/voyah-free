# Voyah Free — General Introduction

The Voyah Free is a premium electric SUV built on Dongfeng’s modular platform.  
It combines a **dual-motor electric drivetrain**, **air suspension**, and an advanced **triple-display infotainment system**.

## Highlights
- Battery capacity: up to 106 kWh (varies by version)
- WLTP range: up to 500 km
- 0–100 km/h: 4.5–4.7 seconds (AWD)
- DC fast charge: ~45 minutes (20–80%)
- Available in **EV** and **REV (range-extended)** versions
- Interior: three integrated screens, adaptive air suspension, and Dynaudio sound system
